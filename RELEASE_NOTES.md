# Release notes
**Hollow Hour v2.0.2: bosses need a word first too**
- Every boss now also needs at least one CHAT (not Check) before RELEASE works, on top of its own rule. MERIDIAN still has to be spent, and now you must also have talked to him once in the fight ("you haven't said a word to me, spark. let go of what?").

**Hollow Hour v2.0.1: talk before you can spare**
- You have to CHAT with an enemy at least once (any option except Check) before RELEASE will let them go. Wearing them down to low HP still works as a mercy path, but only after you have talked. MERIDIAN is unchanged: he still only yields when his spring is spent.
- Tolly's advice in Bellfall mentions the new rule.

**Hollow Hour v2.0: the full clock**
**Hollow Hour v2.0: the full clock**
- 7 endings in all (including the secret THE 12:01), each reachable on its own route; new: THE UNDERSTUDY and the Platform 13 epilogue.
- MERIDIAN now remembers your run: phase intros and callbacks react to who you spared, the tide, the archive, and more.
- MERIDIAN's last phase changes with your route: five different final-phase layers (peace, the 12:01 path, exactly one kill, mixed, violent), each with its own telegraphed pattern.
- Secrets 2.0: new ticket and coat threads tying the town together (no spoilers here).
- Options: Story / Normal / Clockwork, battle speed 70–100%, text speed, hitbox display, high-contrast and colour-blind bullets, reduced flashing/shake, remappable keys, hold-to-confirm.
- Mobile: small-phone layout pass (down to 568×320), larger battle HUD text, haptics, joystick dead zone, 60 FPS battery saver, resume after tab switch.
- Music: every region and boss now has its own theme. New: a quiet theme for Platform 13 and a new wind-down theme for MERIDIAN when he is spent. Listen closely: a few old music-box notes turn up where you might not expect them.
- QA: route bots finish every ending, fairness bots on every attack, random-walk softlock search, save migration from v1.3.
- Boss balance: every boss got a numbers pass (Foreman Grit, the Bilgewater Twins, Madame Cuckoo, Archivist Quill, the Lockkeeper) so the difficulty climbs chapter by chapter. Every attack stays telegraphed and dodgeable, and there are no big PHASE banners mid-fight any more.
- Bosses are still hard. MERIDIAN is still the hardest.
