# Release notes
**Hollow Hour v2.0.4: the hardest hour**
- FULL DIAL no longer hides you. The box now stops just under the top bar, so MERIDIAN's name, the attack counter, the clock and pause sit above the play field. Your name, COIL and GLOW fade under the action instead of covering it, and your soul is always drawn on top. Desktop and phone.
- FULL DIAL is now a real finale. Four hours fire at a time, the bolts are quicker, the HOUR hand joins the minute hand turning the other way, and three chime rings ring out from the middle (always with a gap). It is still telegraphed and always has a way through.
- MERIDIAN is the hardest fight again. He has a new follow-up, CHIME (rings with a gap), after TWELVE STROKES and MIDNIGHT. The attacks toned down in v2.0.3 (REWIND, DESPERATION, KEPT TIME, LITTLE HAMMER) are unchanged.
- The other bosses take less out of you in their longer fights. When a boss repeats an attack it has already used in that fight, the repeat is shorter. The first time you see each attack is unchanged. Archivist Quill's ECHO OF RECORD fires 2 notes at a time (was 3) on a clean record and they fly a little slower.

**Hollow Hour v2.0.3: the full fight**
- Bosses last longer. Boss HP now scales with your COIL and the best weapon you carry, so every boss takes at least 15 perfect CRITICAL hits to beat, even with the strongest gear (the Bilgewater Twins take 8 each). You see every pattern and both forms. Sparing rules are unchanged: talk first, and the low-HP mercy still starts at 25% of the new HP.
- MERIDIAN's new finale, FULL DIAL: after his last attack the box blooms out to the whole screen. His hour marks fire from the edges, a long hand sweeps the middle, and your route's final-phase layer comes along. Then the dial clears and the box shrinks back. Works with keyboard and touch.
- MERIDIAN tone-down on the attacks that were close to impossible: REWIND's spiral can now be crossed (fewer bullets per arm, slower turn, wider wall gaps), DESPERATION's spiral is thinner and turns slower, KEPT TIME rows have wider gaps and more time between them, and LITTLE HAMMER lands less often with a longer 1.8 s fuse and one less shard. He is still the hardest fight.
- Trickle's Bail Out (the circles across the box) keeps the same speed but now warns twice as long and lights up the whole row first.
- A secret dev menu, open to everyone who can find the way in. The old openers are gone. In dev battles you can't lose, and a blue NORMAL HP meter shows what you would have taken (WOULD HAVE DIED at 0).
- Credits: music, sound effects and sprites by Greg; playtesting by Smith.

**Hollow Hour v2.0.2: bosses need a word first too**
- Every boss now also needs at least one CHAT (not Check) before RELEASE works, on top of its own rule. MERIDIAN still has to be spent, and now you must also have talked to him once in the fight ("you haven't said a word to me, spark. let go of what?").

**Hollow Hour v2.0.1: talk before you can spare**
- You have to CHAT with an enemy at least once (any option except Check) before RELEASE will let them go. Wearing them down to low HP still works as a mercy path, but only after you have talked. MERIDIAN is unchanged: he still only yields when his spring is spent.
- Tolly's advice in Bellfall mentions the new rule.

**Hollow Hour v2.0: the full clock**
- 7 endings in all (including the secret THE 12:01), each reachable on its own route; new: THE UNDERSTUDY and the Platform 13 epilogue.
- MERIDIAN now remembers your run: phase intros and callbacks react to who you spared, the tide, the archive, and more.
- MERIDIAN's last phase changes with your route: five different final-phase layers (peace, the 12:01 path, exactly one kill, mixed, violent), each with its own telegraphed pattern.
- Secrets 2.0: new ticket and coat threads tying the town together (no spoilers here).
- Options: Story / Normal / Clockwork, battle speed 70–100%, text speed, hitbox display, high-contrast and colour-blind bullets, reduced flashing/shake, remappable keys, hold-to-confirm.
- Mobile: small-phone layout pass (down to 568×320), larger battle HUD text, haptics, joystick dead zone, 60 FPS battery saver, resume after tab switch.
- Music: every region and boss now has its own theme. New: a quiet theme for Platform 13 and a new wind-down theme for MERIDIAN when he is spent. Listen closely: a few old music-box notes turn up where you might not expect them.
- QA: route bots finish every ending, fairness bots on every attack, random-walk softlock search, save migration from v1.3.
- Boss balance: every boss got a numbers pass (Foreman Grit, the Bilgewater Twins, Madame Cuckoo, Archivist Quill, the Lockkeeper) so the difficulty climbs chapter by chapter. Every attack stays telegraphed and dodgeable, and there are no big PHASE banners mid-fight any more.
- Bosses are still hard. MERIDIAN is still the hardest.
