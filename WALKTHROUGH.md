# HOLLOW HOUR: full walkthrough (v2.0.1, matches the public v2.0.1 build)

Controls: arrows/WASD move · Z/Enter/Space confirm · X/Shift back · Esc pause. On touch: the floating joystick on the left, buttons on the right.
Battle menu: **STRIKE** (attack: stop the needle near the centre) · **CHAT** (ACT options) · **POCKET** (items) · **RELEASE** (Let Go = spare; Slip Away = flee, not available against bosses).
Sparing: you have to CHAT with an enemy at least once (any option except Check) before you can RELEASE them. CHAT options add or remove calm. At 100 calm the name turns gold, and RELEASE → Let Go spares them. Wearing them down to low HP also works, but only after you have talked.
Files: Title → PLAY · FILES gives you three files. Each one shows its name, location, play time, and endings. CONTINUE, NEW (asks before overwriting), DELETE. A save from before v1.3 shows up as File 1. CREDITS is on the title screen and on every end screen (BACK or Esc returns).

## 1. The main path (every route)
1. **Bellfall Clearing**: talk to TOLLY, then use the chime post (save). Exit east.
2. **Rustwood Trail**: **Burrlock** stands on the main path and charges when you get close (you always fight him). Other monsters wander in plain sight and chase you if you get near (no random encounters); touch one to fight it. Exit east.
3. **Lantern Grove**: light the lanterns **right → middle → left** (n2, n1, n0). The vines open. North.
4. **The Candle Gate**: chime post (save + full heal) at (12,12). Walk north to fight **WICKLOW**.
5. **Gearholm** (town: shop, inn, NPCs). North.
6. **The Cogworks**: you will always meet **Spannerkin** here. Crate puzzle: push the left crate 8 tiles left, then up onto the top-left plate. Push the right crate 8 tiles right, then up onto the top-right plate. The reset lever is bottom-left. North.
7. **Foreman's Floor**: chime post at (12,12). Walk north to fight **FOREMAN GRIT**.
8. **Hourspire Stair**: you will always meet **Metronoma** here. Lever puzzle: the sign says "UP · DOWN · UP" and the levers are linked to their neighbours. Keep flipping until the pattern is up/down/up (any order of flips that reaches that pattern works). North.
9. **Cuckoo Gallery**: chime post at (12,12). Walk north to fight **MADAME CUCKOO**.
10. **The Belfry**: chime post at (12,12) (save + full heal). Walk up to meet **MERIDIAN**, then talk to him to start the 5-phase final fight (16 attacks + combo follow-ups).

Save points (all of them heal fully): Bellfall (10,11), Rustwood (24,11), Candle Gate (12,12), Gearholm (24,12), Foreman's Floor (12,12), Cuckoo Gallery (12,12), Belfry (12,12). Optional areas: Tidelock: the Low Steps (20,7), Weir Stairs (25,4), Keeper's Walk (26,4); Undercroft: Hall of Filed Clocks (24,10), Reading Room (15,8).

**Side quest: Ferry's Fare (v1.7, Tidelock, optional)**. Talk to **Ferry** at the Low Steps a few times. On your third chat he asks for a fare, and it has to be "something from before". Go to the **Weir Stairs**. Once the quest has started, the **Mooring Tag** is caught on the last step at (27,9). Bring it back to Ferry. He rows one lap the other way and gives you the **Lap Knot** (armor, PLATING +5). If your pockets are full, he holds it until you make room. No ending depends on this quest.

**Side quest: Pell's Cue (Hourspire, optional; needed for THE UNDERSTUDY)**. From the **Hourspire Stair** east wall, enter **the Weightworks**. Set the three track switches to **MAIN, BRANCH, MAIN** and ring the dispatch bell; the car reaches the lift and the bars to **the Understudy's Loft** go up. Talk to **PELL** once. Open the Loft chest for the **Cue Bell** (carry it or wear it), then talk to Pell again: Pell waits in the Gallery wings. In the MADAME CUCKOO fight, once she is half calm (or after Hum Along), CHAT → **Ring the Cue Bell**. It becomes a duet; RELEASE → Let Go to finish it. Do this before fighting Cuckoo and not on a violent run. Killing Cuckoo after the cue loses it.

| Rustpup | Rustwood | Pet, then Throw Bolt (or Oil Joints twice) |

Killing: STRIKE until it falls. Each region has a fixed population (9-10). Once you have killed them all, the grass goes quiet.

## 3. Bosses
- **WICKLOW** (Candle Knight): Salute, Shield Flame, Ask About Gate → RELEASE. Finale "Last Light": the box goes dark except around you.
- **FOREMAN GRIT**: Work Hard / Show Clock / Suggest Break → RELEASE. Finale "The Rules" (keep moving; left side only; obey the belt).
- **MADAME CUCKOO**: Applaud / Hum Along / Request Encore → RELEASE. Finale "Your Song" replays your journey. With Pell's Cue set up, **Ring the Cue Bell** turns her solo into a duet (see Pell's Cue above).
- **THE BILGEWATER TWINS** (Tidelock, Twin Sluices; optional mid-boss): two bosses, one battle. SLOSH: Bail Together / Agree With Slosh / Admire Bucket. TRICKLE: Hold Ladle / Agree With Trickle / Read the Rota. Calm both, then RELEASE. If one falls, the one left behind needs Hold the Other Door / Sit With Them / Say Sorry. Any outcome lets you continue.
- **THE LOCKKEEPER** (Tidelock, tower; optional): Read the Logbook, Say the Count (only lands if Ferry told you the number), Point at the LOW, then He Could Stop → RELEASE. **Turn the Wheel** drops the tide to LOW (needed for LOW TIDE). Attacks: Sluice Slats, Tide Marks, Ferry Laps, The Wheel (stand in the marked gap). If he drops to half HP before he is fully calm, he becomes HIGH WATER: a harder second form, and his calm is halved.
- **ARCHIVIST QUILL** (Hourspire Undercroft: Archive Stair → Hall of Filed Clocks → Stacks → Reading Room, then north; optional): Read Your Entry, then Correct the Record, Ask What It's For, and Sign It once it's calm enough → RELEASE. If it drops to half HP before it is fully calm, it starts VOL. 2: a harder second form, and its calm is halved (no banner; the music changes).
- **MERIDIAN**: you can't calm him. Survive all 16 attacks (in phases 4-5, two out of every three attacks are followed straight away by a COMBO). Use CHAT (Ask Why / Laugh at Joke) to pass turns. When he is **spent**: RELEASE → Let Go spares him, and STRIKE ends him. Optional: Wind His Key while spent → *One More Minute*. Show Ticket and Show Photo appear if you carry those items. MERIDIAN remembers your run: he opens with a line about your route, mentions up to two things you did on the way in the next phases, may bring one more up when phase 5 starts, and says one last thing when he winds down (max 3 lines per fight). **Phase 5 depends on your route**: an extra layer goes on top of each of the last four attacks. Peace = *Kept Time* (gold pendulum rows with one swinging gap); peace with the ticket = *One Minute Past* (a long minute hand sweeps clockwise from 12:01); exactly one kill = *One Stopped Clock* (a slow grey homing clock); mixed = *Can't Decide* (two hands turning opposite ways); violent = *Little Hammer* (a countdown bomb lands where you stand and bursts into a ring; move off it). Every layer is telegraphed. Practice and Boss Rush use the plain phase 5. No PHASE banners: watch the music and the arena instead.

## 4. Routes and endings
Route rule: **peace** = 0 kills. **violent** = all 3 bosses killed AND at least 12 kills. Anything else is **mixed**.
| Ending | How |
|---|---|
| THE TOWN WINDS ON (true) | Peace route, spare MERIDIAN |
| MIDNIGHT, AT LAST (quiet) | Mixed or violent route, spare MERIDIAN (when no special ending below applies) |
| A CRACKED BELL | Mixed route, kill MERIDIAN |
| THE STILL HOUR | Violent route (kill Wicklow, Grit, Cuckoo, and at least 12 total; grind Rustwood and the Cogworks), kill MERIDIAN |
| <details><summary>LOW TIDE (v1.7)</summary>Not violent. In the Tidelock (Gearholm's east wall): in the Turning Basin pull the WEST lever (LOW, cross the stones), MIDDLE lever (HIGH, cross the pontoon), EAST lever (LOW, Lock No. 2 opens). Long Lock → Weir Stairs → Twin Sluices (any Twins outcome) → Keeper's Walk → tower door. SPARE the Lockkeeper with the tide LOW (ACT Turn the Wheel, then calm him). Then finish the game and spare MERIDIAN. On a peace route you get the "all hands" version. 12:01 still takes priority if you show the ticket.</details> | |
| <details><summary>THE UNDERSTUDY (v1.8)</summary>Mixed route (at least one kill, not violent), finish **Pell's Cue** with the duet spare of Madame Cuckoo, then spare MERIDIAN. LOW TIDE and 12:01 take priority. On a peace run the duet doesn't change your ending.</details> | |
| <details><summary>THE 12:01 (hidden)</summary>Peace route, and after Grit go back to Gearholm, enter the Clockmaker's (door at 32,8), set the drawer to **12 : :01** to get Tomorrow's Ticket, then use CHAT → **Show Ticket** on MERIDIAN and spare him. Its end screen has a small **PLATFORM 13** button: a short walk-around epilogue (no fights, no saving). Talk to everyone, then sit on the bench at the end of the platform. It only opens straight after earning THE 12:01.</details> | |

7 endings in all. If more than one fits, the order is: THE 12:01 → LOW TIDE → THE UNDERSTUDY → true / quiet. Killing MERIDIAN always gives A CRACKED BELL or THE STILL HOUR.

Post-ending changes (the crater tag, Mopsley at the crater, the 12:01 save-post time) are tracked **per file**: they show up in a new game started in a file that has already seen an ending. The endings gallery and the records are **global** (shared by all files).

16. **Ledger No. 14**: Hall of Filed Clocks (26,9). Read the Foreman's Floor ledger first.
